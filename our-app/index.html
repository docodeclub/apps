Today

Know what's happening and who's doing it

Open Today to see what's next, who's handling it and what needs attention. See who's picking up from football, whether the swimming bag is ready and what's coming up tomorrow.

If plans change, update the collection and give the other adult a chance to confirm. Everyone can check the same plan.

For the things a family shares

Calendar & collections

A calendar that works around family life

See school term dates, inset days, clubs, appointments, birthdays, days out and date nights together. Filter by family member or category when you want a simpler view.

Set a club to repeat during term time. Give drop-off and collection their own times, locations and responsible adults. If Wednesday's pickup changes, update that day and keep the rest of the term's plan in place.

Shared care

When someone's unwell, make the handover easier

Keep a shared record of medicine already given: the product and strength, how much was given, when and by whom. Save frequently used products and confirm the amount and time each time you log medicine.

Add temperatures, symptoms and care notes as the day goes on. Another adult can check the latest shared record when they take over. You choose who can access each child's care information.

Offline records are marked as waiting to be shared. Reminders are separate from records of medicine given, and you choose when to set them.

Shopping

A shopping list that updates while you shop

Start a shop and open the same list your family can add to from home. If someone remembers the bananas while you're in the supermarket, they appear when you're connected.

Tick items into In the basket as you go, and undo a tick if you change your mind. Anything you didn't buy stays on the list when you finish.

Keep separate lists for the weekly shop, pharmacy or DIY store. Paste in a list from WhatsApp, add quantities and notes, and bring back regular buys with Buy again.

Tasks & packing

Give everyday jobs a home

Share packing lists, assign tasks and set reminders. Put the swimming kit, school form and birthday present where the person handling them can find them.

Start on your own or invite your family

Create a family space and add your first plan or list. When you're ready, invite another adult to join with their own account. Our App is designed for parents, guardians and families of different shapes and sizes.

Questions about Our App

Can I use Our App on my own?

Yes. Start with your own calendar, care records and lists, then invite another adult whenever you're ready.

Does everyone in the family see my child's care records?

You choose which adults can access each child's care information. An invitation to the family calendar and lists is separate from permission to view care records.

Does the app tell me when or how much medicine to give?

Care is for recording medicine you've already given and setting reminders at times you choose. For dose and timing instructions, follow the medicine label or your clinician's advice.

Can someone add to the shopping list while I'm at the shop?

Yes. When you're both connected, new items appear automatically. If you lose reception, you can keep using your saved list and see which changes are waiting to sync.

Which devices will it be available on?

The first release is planned for iPhone.
