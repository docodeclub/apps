# ClubNight Product Brief

ClubNight is the strongest follow-up idea to FrameScore.

## Core Thesis

FrameScore solves the table-side scoring problem for snooker. ClubNight solves the room-side organisation problem for clubs: who is here, who is next, which table is free, what match is happening, and where the results go afterwards.

The product should not start as a generic social app. It should start as a practical session board for real club nights.

## Target Users

- snooker club organisers
- pool league captains
- darts night organisers
- table tennis and squash club volunteers
- players who want a fair queue and visible match order

## First Pain to Solve

Most small club nights are coordinated through a mix of:

- paper sheets
- WhatsApp messages
- memory
- spreadsheets
- one organiser answering the same question repeatedly

ClubNight should make the answer visible:

> Who is here, who plays next, where do they play, and what happened?

## MVP

- create a club session
- add/check in players
- create tables, boards or courts
- maintain a visible queue
- start a match
- enter a quick result
- show session history
- export results
- link a snooker match to FrameScore

## Why It Fits Docode

- practical workflow
- clear user problem
- obvious first niche
- credible extension from FrameScore
- useful without needing a big network
- can launch small and deepen over time

## Risks

- clubs may resist subscriptions
- organisers may already tolerate messy workflows
- sport-specific rules can create scope creep
- too much social/community design would dilute the product

## Validation Plan

1. Interview five club organisers.
2. Watch one real club night.
3. Prototype session board, queue and result entry.
4. Test with one snooker club using FrameScore as the match scoring layer.
5. Decide whether the first paid product is per-club, per-organiser or per-venue.

## Strong Positioning

ClubNight is not “sports club management software”.

ClubNight is:

> The live board for local club nights.

That is the wedge.
