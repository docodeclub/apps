Docode.app Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Docode.app is the website for Docode Limited. This policy explains how the website handles personal data, browser storage and contact information.

Summary Data Storage Rights Contact

Summary

  • Docode.app does not use analytics, advertising cookies or tracking cookies
  • Docode.app does not sell personal data
  • Docode.app does not use a custom login
  • the website only uses essential browser storage to remember that the privacy notice has been dismissed

Personal Data We May Receive

If you contact Docode Limited by email or through a contact link, we may receive the information you choose to send. This may include your name, email address, message content, device details, app support information or project enquiry details.

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, provide support, manage business communication and keep appropriate records.

Cookies and Browser Storage

Docode.app does not use analytics, advertising cookies or tracking cookies.

The website uses one essential local browser storage preference named docodeCookieNoticeSeen. This remembers that you have dismissed the privacy notice so it is not shown repeatedly.

This browser storage stays on your device. You can remove it by clearing site data for Docode.app in your browser settings.

Sharing and Third Parties

Docode.app is hosted as a static website. Website hosting providers may process basic technical information such as IP address, request time and requested page as part of delivering the site and protecting the service.

Docode Limited does not sell personal data and does not add third-party advertising, analytics or tracking scripts to this website.

How Long We Keep Data

Contact messages and support enquiries are kept only for as long as needed to respond, provide support, manage records and meet any legal or business requirements.

Your Rights

Depending on your location and the circumstances, you may have rights to ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to the use of your personal data.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office if you are concerned about how personal data is handled.
