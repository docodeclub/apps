FrameScore Help

iPad snooker scoring guide.

A practical guide to scoring frames, tracking breaks, handling fouls and reviewing match history in FrameScore.

Set Up a Match

Choose two players, select a match length and confirm whether the table should auto-end at the target. FrameScore is designed for iPad landscape use, so the scoreboard and controls stay readable beside the table.

Score Reds and Colours

Use the pot ball controls to score reds, colours and multiple reds. FrameScore keeps track of points, reds remaining, frame state and the current break while the scorer stays in control.

Record Fouls

Use the foul controls when a player misses, touches a ball or concedes penalty points. FrameScore records fouls and foul points so the frame summary remains useful after play.

Track Breaks and Timers

The live scoreboard shows the current break, frame time and match time. This helps players review pace, scoring patterns and practice sessions after the match.

Review Match History

After a match, use history and summaries to review frame results, points scored, balls potted, scoring pots, fouls and player stats.
