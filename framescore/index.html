FrameScore

Snooker Scoring & Break Tracker for iPad.

FrameScore is a clean, touch-first snooker scoring app designed for real play.

Support Privacy Policy

Built for amateur players, practice frames and club matches, FrameScore gives you a polished iPad scoreboard with fast scoring controls, player stats, match history and smart snooker flow.

FrameScore is made for table-side scoring. It helps keep the match organised while leaving the scorer in control.

Features

  • Fast two-player snooker scoring
  • Red and colour scoring flow
  • Current break tracking
  • Fouls, concessions and re-racks
  • Frame and match timers
  • Player profiles and stats
  • Match history and summaries
  • iCloud sync support where available
  • Designed for iPad landscape

Match Flow

1

Set Up Players

Choose players, match length and table settings before the frame starts.

2

Score Quickly

Tap reds, colours, fouls and frame controls from a table-side layout.

3

Review the Match

Use summaries, player stats and history to keep track of performance.
