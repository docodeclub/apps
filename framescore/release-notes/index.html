FrameScore

Release Notes

Updates and improvements for FrameScore, the iPad snooker scoring and break tracking app.

Initial App Store Release

FrameScore launches with touch-first snooker scoring for iPad, match setup, player profiles, break tracking, fouls, concessions, re-racks, frame timers, match timers, history and summaries.

What Comes Next

Future updates will focus on improving scoring flow, match review, player statistics and reliability based on real table-side use.
