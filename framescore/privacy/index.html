FrameScore Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 May 2026

FrameScore is an iPad snooker scoring app by Docode Limited.

Data Stored by the App

FrameScore may store information needed to provide scoring, match history, player profiles and statistics. This may include:

  • player names
  • nicknames
  • age
  • handedness
  • email address, if entered
  • match history
  • frame results
  • scoring statistics
  • app settings

How Data Is Used

This data is used only to provide app functionality, including scoring, match history, player statistics and iCloud sync where available.

Local Storage and iCloud

FrameScore stores data locally on the device. If iCloud sync is available and enabled, match and player data may also be stored in the user's private iCloud account using Apple CloudKit.

No Advertising or Tracking

FrameScore does not use advertising tracking.

FrameScore does not sell user data.

FrameScore does not require a custom login.

Data Deletion

Users can delete match history, reset player statistics and clear app data from within the app settings where available.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact:
